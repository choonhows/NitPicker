# 2016S_FE_AM_18
Which of the following is the mean time between failures (MTBF) value of the entire
system where unit A and unit B are connected in series and both units operate as one system?
The MTBF value of unit A and unit B is 200 and 300 hours, respectively.

a) 200
b) 250
c) 300
d) 500
?
b) 250
