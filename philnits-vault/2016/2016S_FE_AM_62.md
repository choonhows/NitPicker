# 2016S_FE_AM_62
Among the items to be written into an audit report by a system auditor, which of the
following deviates from the scope of work of an auditor?

a) Judging the urgency of improvements
b) Ordering improvements
c) Pointing out improvements
d) Recommending improvements
?
b) Ordering improvements
