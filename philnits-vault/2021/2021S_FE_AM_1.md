# 2021S_FE_AM_1
Which of the following is equivalent to the logic expression $(x + y) \cdot (x + z)$? Here, the letters are logic variables; $x + y$, $x \cdot y$, and $\overline{x}$ are OR, AND, and NOT operations on the corresponding variables, respectively.

a) $x \cdot (y + z)$
b) $x + y \cdot z$
c) $x \cdot y + y \cdot z$
d) $(\overline{x} + y) \cdot z$
?
b) $x + y \cdot z$
