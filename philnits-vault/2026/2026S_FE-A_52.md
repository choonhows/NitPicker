# 2026S_FE-A_52
When trial production is performed for components to be loaded into electrical appliances, which of the following is the least expensive development method? Here, the construction time of each process is six (6) months during the creation process, three (3) months during the modification process, and two (2) months during the evaluation process. The cost per one (1) person-month is 6,000 dollars during the creation process and 10,000 dollars for each modification and evaluation process. Costs other than the purchasing, outsourcing, and person-month are not considered.

| Development method | Purchase cost (dollars) | Outsourcing cost (dollars) | Creation process (persons per month) | Modification process (persons per month) | Evaluation process (persons per month) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purchasing samples and modifying them in-house | 200,000 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 1 |
| Outsourcing in batches | 0 | 350,000 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Modifying in-house assets | 0 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 3 |
| Creating new products in-house | 0 | 0 | 10 | 0 | 2 |

a) Purchasing samples and modifying them in-house
b) Outsourcing in batches
c) Modifying in-house assets
d) Creating new products in-house
?
a) Purchasing samples and modifying them in-house
