# 2026S_FE-A_14
Which of the following is the CPU utilization in percentage rounded to a decimal
number when three (3) jobs with 30% average I/O (Input/Output) wait time are running on a
single CPU? Here, I/O wait time refers to the percentage of time a job is waiting for its I/O
operation to complete.

a) 10
b) 34
c) 90
d) 97
?
d) 97
